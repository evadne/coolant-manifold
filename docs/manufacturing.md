**Assembly is separate:** [Q assembly guide](assembly-Q.md) and [radiator assembly guide](radiator-rack-plate.md). The fabrication PDFs do not set screw torque, apply threadlocker or order bought-in hardware.

The long galleries remain the principal CNC capability review item. Black unfilled declared POM-C or POM-H is accepted in principle; JLC must identify its offered stock. Both2 mm stainless plates use sheet-metal fabrication, with finishing where required to meet the drawings. Matching M4 hole coordinates are±0.05 on both manifold parts; other port/window centres±0.10. All20 boss roots retain positive nominal/tolerance clearance to the windows.

Earlier P review files and O-M02 supplier ZIPs are historical. Do not mix their16 mm M4 threads/18 mm pilots or old countersunk plate details into Q-M01. R6-M01 is superseded by the explicit finish issue, with no radiator geometry change.
