# JLCCNC first article observations

This report concerns the Q-M04 POM body, Q-M03 manifold faceplate and R7-M01 radiator plate supplied by **JLCCNC**, as reported by the operator on 5 October 2026. It distinguishes this batch's workmanship from the design's general requirements. It is not a judgement of other suppliers or a blanket acceptance of every surface on this sample.

| Item | Operator finding |
|---|---|
| Manifold body and faceplate dimensions | Correct |
| All POM M4 screw holes and G1/4 ports | Correct |
| QD3 threading | Smooth |
| POM corner detail | Good; accepted |
| 2 mm faceplate thickness | Mounts without perceptible flex |
| POM holding marks | Acceptable where concealed by the faceplate |
| Steel edges | Slight sharpness; adequacy of delivered deburring remains open |
| Radiator plate | Delivered; operator-unverified until a spare Supernova is available |

![Manifold received from JLCCNC with one fitted coupling](references/first-article-2026-10-05/01-assembled-qd3.jpg)

The stainless faceplate hides some POM blemishes and becomes the principal visible surface. Its appearance and tactile finish therefore need consistent control. The reported sharpness suggests that standard deburring as delivered may fall short of the desired handling quality. Exact edges and severity have not been characterised; a supplier nonconformance has not been established.

Q-M03 explicitly accepted standard grinding/deburring without guaranteed complete burr removal. That concession belongs to the manifold faceplate. R7-M01 separately requires cable-contact edge rounding; it has not been waived. A repeat order should distinguish reproducing this accepted pack from requesting an improved finishing operation. Matching raw finish remains selected for both plates; no brushing or polishing change has been approved.

These are first-hand operator observations, not a supplied metrology certificate. No leak-test result, radiator fit result or new pressure/load/creep qualification has been reported. The [order chronology and photographs](jlc-order-2026-09-16.md#first-article-observations--5-october-2026) are the underlying record. Supplier files remain unchanged.
