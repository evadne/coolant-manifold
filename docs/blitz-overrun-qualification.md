# Robustness to an overdue Blitz Part 2 flush

Date: 15 September 2026. Status: design requirement and proposed qualification plan; no exposure tests performed.

## Product requirement

The operator explicitly requires tolerance of users forgetting to drain Mayhems Blitz Part 2 / Blitz System on time. The manifold must not rely on exact adherence to the cleaner's instructed duration to avoid leakage, cracking or loss of fitting retention. Normal-use instructions remain distinct from the product's required resistance to foreseeable misuse.

Both POM-C and POM-H remain candidates. Acceptance for normal computer coolant up to approximately 50°C does not yet demonstrate resistance to extended cleaner exposure. Neither polymer family is selected or rejected by this new requirement alone.

## Initial qualification targets

These durations are engineering proposals to make the requirement testable, not operator-specified durations or claimed product ratings:

| Exposure | Purpose | Temperature |
|---|---|---|
| Manufacturer's instructed cycle | Baseline comparison | Pump-only conditions, recorded |
| 72 hours | Missed drain / weekend interruption | 23°C and 50°C |
| 7 days | Initial minimum robustness target | 23°C and 50°C |
| 30 days | Extended robustness target and material comparison | 23°C and 50°C |

Use the prescribed dilution for the actual bottle/version, and record product lot, mixture and initial/final pH. An incorrect concentrate ratio is a separate case, not implied to be covered by these timing tests. The 50°C cleaner exposures deliberately represent foreseeable misuse, not recommended cleaning practice. Do not convert an exposure at 50°C into an equivalent number of years by assumption.

Test both stagnant filled assemblies and circulating assemblies. Include thermal cycling after exposure and after flushing/refilling with the chosen coolant. Maintain liquid concentration during heated tests rather than unintentionally testing evaporative concentration. Use specimens from the actual proposed black unfilled POM stock and identify manufacturer, grade, lot and stock form. If both C and H are to remain permitted production substitutions, qualify representative stock for each.

## Test articles and evidence

Screen machined, stressed port specimens before full-body trials. Include the 4 mm bosses, actual G1/4 threads, sealing lands and selected fittings/plug seals, together with representative M4 retention preload. Simple unstressed plastic coupons cannot establish loaded-thread or assembly behaviour. Compare with unexposed controls and coolant-exposed controls from the same stock; use multiple specimens rather than relying on a single successful sample.

Full-assembly acceptance must cover:

- No external leakage during exposure or after flushing, coolant refill and thermal cycling.
- No cracking, crazing, thread stripping, fitting loosening or seal-land damage that impairs retention or sealing.
- Functional plate/boss fit and repeatable sealing after fitting removal/reinstallation.
- Retained pressure and fitting-retention capability against predetermined design minima, compared with controls. Record dimensional, mass and mechanical changes so apparently sound surfaces do not conceal degradation.

The numerical working/proof pressure, fitting torque, retention load, instrument sensitivity and sample count must be established before execution from the selected pump arrangement, hardware and product reliability objective. They are not already qualified in Revision O. Tests must include the actual sealing elastomers and wetted metals; body survival alone is insufficient. Failure to meet a target requires a material/stock/seal/design change or reconsideration of the target, not merely a shorter user warning.

## Limits of the published evidence

Mayhems' instructions establish a normal cleaning procedure, not a POM failure threshold or an overrun lifetime. Its product page says the cleaner is not a long-term inhibitor; this also means POM compatibility alone cannot qualify the rest of a mixed-material loop. [Instructions](https://mayhems.store/pub/media/pdf/mayhems_instructions_blitzkit.pdf), [product information](https://mayhems.store/mayhems-blitz-complete-radiator-and-system-cleaning-kit.html)

The July 2024 standalone Blitz System SDS is internally inconsistent: its description calls the product pH-neutral and lists detergent ingredients, while section 9 reports an acidic pH at a specified high concentration. The combined-kit SDS repeats this discrepancy. We cannot resolve it by assuming which entry is correct, or treat either figure as the verified pH of the use dilution. Obtain clarified formulation/use-dilution information for the tested lot and measure the mixture; no manufacturer enquiry has been sent. [Blitz System SDS](https://mayhems.store/pub/media/pdf/mayhems_msds_blitz_system.pdf), [combined SDS](https://mayhems.store/pub/media/pdf/mayhems_msds_blitz_complete.pdf)

The present conclusion is that delayed draining must be covered by product qualification. The available evidence does not yet establish seven- or thirty-day survival for either POM grade in this assembled manifold. Existing supplier drawings are machining documents and remain unchanged; they do not claim these exposure ratings.
