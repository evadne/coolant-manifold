# MO-RA IV tank mounting reference review

> Historical MO-RA mounting reference. The current cooling plan uses SuperNova/R6; see [the current StarTech25U plan](../context-25U.md).


Reviewed 15 September 2026 after the operator rejected the invented rear-offset tank carrier. This corrects the visual mounting arrangement, without selecting a stock radiator for the custom 10U installation.

## Sources inspected

- [Watercool Tank 200 D5 product photographs and dimensions](https://shop.watercool.de/MO-RA-IV-Tank-200-D5_1): inspected the enlarged product photo in the browser. The rectangular acrylic-front tank and lower pump cavity form a slim assembly, with a published body envelope of 275 ×84 ×49 mm.
- [Watercool MO-RA IV 400 product photographs](https://shop.watercool.de/MO-RA-IV-400-Black_1): inspected the enlarged radiator photo, showing the broad fin/fan face and narrow connection-side panel. This product's dimensions are not substituted into the custom rack radiator.
- [Watercool tank mounting manual](https://shop.watercool.de/mediafiles/Manuals/MA_MO-RA_IV_TANK.pdf), printed pages 3–5: visually inspected the assembly diagrams. The upper tank rear port mates directly to an adapter on the narrow radiator side. A compact retainer locates below the reservoir, with additional rear screws. The D5 lower part joins beneath the reservoir; the motor projects outward from the same side-facing plane as the window. Top access is provided by two fill plugs.

The installed orientation comes from the manual diagrams; the product photographs establish appearance and the radiator's narrow-side geometry. Do not describe an invented behind-the-fin carrier as manufacturer mounting.

## Application to the rack model

- Preserve the custom 440 ×440 mm radiator face and 10U allocation.
- Rotate the tank/window/pump arrangement to the narrow right-hand side. Remove the large rear carrier and long radiator-to-tank hose. Represent a direct upper fluid adapter and compact lower retention.
- Use the 275 ×84 ×49 mm tank body reference. Motor projection, small fastening details and custom radiator port coordinates remain illustrative.
- Recess the radiator body an additional 60 mm behind its rack ears to clear the front post. Short stand-offs connect the front ears to the radiator frame; this is a custom rack detail, not stock Watercool hardware.
- Explicitly retain the outboard width consequence: the pump envelope reaches X+336 mm versus the rack post outer edge near X+267 mm. A 440 mm radiator plus this side module is not contained inside the nominal 450 mm equipment opening. The current view demonstrates mounting and its packaging impact; it does not establish a drop-in rack fit.

MO-RA3 has different reservoir/module mounting accessories. This rendition uses the MO-RA IV Tank arrangement as its visual reference; do not conflate their attachment details. Manifold O-M02 geometry, upper rack allocations and GPU connection orientation are unchanged.
