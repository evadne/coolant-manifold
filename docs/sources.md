# Design references

Reviewed 14 September 2026. Manufacturer drawings are references, not our own deliverables or specifications of a completed assembly.

- [EK Pro portfolio — 2CPU 8GPU, page31](https://www.ekwb.com/shop/mediaset/EK-PRO-Portfolio-2025-Q4.pdf): published dimensions326 ×57 ×36 mm and acetal/stainless construction. Exact port pitch not verified. The user confirms EK spacing is usable with QD3.
- [EK manifold user guide](https://www.ekwb.com/shop/EK-IM/EK-IM-3831109817209.pdf): manufacturer's configurable parallel/semi-parallel topology; our design omits its optional grouping. Text was accessible through web search; direct download was blocked.
- [Koolance QD3-MTG4 drawing](https://koolance.com/files/products/diagrams/qd3-mtg4_d1.pdf): current male fitting dimensions. Cached locally under references/qd3-mtg4.pdf.
- [Koolance QD3-MSG4 drawing](https://koolance.com/files/products/diagrams/qd3-msg4_d1.pdf): earlier male version, cached under references/qd3-msg4.pdf. The [old product page](https://koolance.com/qd3-msg4-quick-disconnect-no-spill-coupling-male-threaded-g-1-4) identifies MTG4 as its replacement.
- [Koolance QD3-FS10X16 drawing](https://koolance.com/files/products/diagrams/qd3-fs10x16_d1.pdf): example hose-side female fitting, cached under references/qd3-fs10x16.pdf. The actual intended female variant remains to be chosen.
- [Völkel G1/4 ×19 tap](https://voelkel.com/en/machine-tap-din-5156-form-b-hsse-g-bsp-1-4-x-19/78514): 11.8 mm core-hole recommendation. [Gühring catalogue](https://guhring.com/media/catalogs/fuomh0a2doj.pdf) gives BSP tooling/thread data. G1/4 nominal major diameter is13.157 mm, not the16.662 mm of G3/8.
- [Penn Elcom rack panel drawing](https://www.farnell.com/datasheets/1843559.pdf):19-inch panel dimensions; [Keysight rack guide](https://docs.rs-online.com/4a5b/0900766b80e686f6.pdf) supplies nominal rack opening and mounting dimensions. One U is44.45 mm; use actual rail checks before release.
- [Parker O-Ring Handbook](https://www.parker.com/content/dam/Parker-com/Literature/O-Ring-Division-Literature/ORD-5700.pdf): static face-seal design methodology. Revision A gland calculations are our proposed dimensions, not a validated Parker seal selection.
- [Xometry acetal CNC machining](https://www.xometry.com/capabilities/cnc-machining-service/acetal/): acetal/Delrin capability, creep and stock-porosity considerations. [JLCCNC capabilities](https://jlccnc.com/): lists POM and conventional CNC/sheet-metal services. Neither supplier has reviewed or quoted this design.

The reference drawings support component envelopes. They do not establish assembled QD overlap, release-ring stroke, comfortable hand clearance, manifold pressure rating or flow performance.
