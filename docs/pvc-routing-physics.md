# Firm, pliant PVC routing

The 25U scene's eighteen front branches now use a static elastic-rod calculation. The previous straight spans joined by circular bends were useful for clearance planning but looked like rigid pipe. The new curves distribute bending continuously and settle under their own weight and the contained coolant. Front-view review exposed an error in the first implementation: it prescribed every lateral X coordinate, so only Y/Z settled. This restriction has been removed. All free-span coordinates now participate in the calculation, with paired-tube contact preventing the hoses passing through each other. Following operator review of the first relaxed side view, each front branch is shortened by100 mm in nominal modelled length and solved again. The three10/16 infrastructure runs retain their previous nominal geometry.

## Material estimate

[Koolance HOS-10CL-3M](https://koolance.com/tubing-clear-uv-reactive-pvc-10mm-x-13mm-3-8in-x-1-2in-3m) specifies 10 mm ID, 13 mm OD, Shore 80A hardness, approximately 37 mm bend radius and 0.27 kg product weight per 3 m. The latter is used as a dry-mass approximation, not a separately measured tubing density.

Tube bending stiffness is **E I**, where `I = pi (OD^4 - ID^4) / 64`. For 10/13 tubing, I is 911.11 mm⁴. Wall thickness alone fixes I, but does not fix the compound's elastic modulus E.

The [Gent hardness correlation reproduced in this research paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8849984/) gives `E [MPa] = 0.0981 (56 + 7.66 S) / [0.137505 (254 - 2.54 S)]`. At S = 80, this gives approximately **9.4 MPa**, and EI approximately **8,564 N mm²**. This elastomer correlation is applied to flexible PVC as a first approximation; it is not a measured constitutive law for Koolance's formulation. It provides a reasoned starting point instead of an arbitrary softness control. Temperature, loading time and coiling memory are not inferred from hardness.

The contained coolant density is assumed to be 1,000 kg/m³. Combined with the dry-mass approximation, the filled tube weighs 0.16854 kg/m, giving a gravitational load of 0.0016534 N/mm. These inputs are explicit in [the parameters](../cad/context/pvc-routing.json).

Mayhems' [Ultra Flex 11/16 page](https://mayhems.store/mayhems-soft-tubing-ultraflex-1116-3m.html) describes polymer additives, high flexibility, absence of phthalates and resistance to plasticiser leaching. It supplies no numerical hardness, modulus or minimum bend radius on the reviewed page. These claims do not identify the formulation or establish the same stiffness as Koolance. The linked tube has a 2.5 mm wall and is not the 10/13 tube fitted to QD3-FT10X13. No Mayhems material substitution is made.

## Calculation and limits

[The solver](../scripts/relax_context_tubes.py) minimises axial strain energy, discrete bending energy and gravitational potential with an analytic gradient and Hessian. Each branch uses 101 centre-line nodes. **X, Y and Z are free between the fitting exits.** Only approximately 9 mm at each fitting is clamped to its attachment axis. There are no lateral guides or hidden supports along the free spans.

Each supply/return pair is solved together using frictionless, regularised contact. The former lateral routes provide an initial left/right passing order only; they exert no force and do not constrain the final curve. Contact uses a quadratic repulsion below 14 mm node separation: 13 mm tube OD plus a 1 mm numerical allowance for discrete nodes and interpolation. The 10 N/mm penalty stiffness is a numerical contact setting, not measured PVC contact stiffness. This makes smooth bows where paired hoses pass each other instead of forcing a sideways transition near every fitting. Contact is solved within each pair; separation between pairs and from equipment is checked afterwards. It is not an all-equipment contact simulation, and a failed clearance check stops the build.

This is a straight-rest-rod approximation with fixed fitting positions and nominal branch lengths reduced by 100 mm. The resulting modelled GPU branch lengths are approximately 467.5/457.4 mm, and host branches 447.4/449.1 mm; these are centre-line lengths between modelled attachment points, not a workshop cut list including insertion allowances. It does not identify plasticiser chemistry, model residual coiling, internal pressure, ovalisation, friction, thermal softening or long-term creep. A handling video could help judge spring-back and coiling memory, but cannot uniquely identify formulation or modulus without known forces and dimensions.

[Blender soft bodies](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/physics/soft_body/settings/edges.html) offer edge springs and bending resistance, with [goal constraints](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/physics/soft_body/settings/goal.html) for attachments. Those controls need calibration; they do not directly encode a measured PVC material. This scene therefore uses a reproducible static rod solution calculated in Python, then builds the annular PVC/coolant meshes and renders them in Blender. There is no native soft-body bake presented as a calibrated simulation.

## Results and verification

- The shortened GPU branches settle downward by approximately 10.35–11.86 mm from the initial guess; host branches by 20.00–20.22 mm. These are solver displacements from the seed, not sag measurements from a straight support chord or the previous longer tubes.
- Minimum GPU bend radius is approximately 70.84 mm, and the host minimum is 70.01 mm; infrastructure remains at 65 mm. Existing check floors remain 50 mm for GPUs and 40 mm for the host.
- The minimum conservative tube-to-tube surface gap is approximately 0.31 mm at a paired-hose contact region, after subtracting the sampling allowance. This small positive model gap comes from the numerical contact allowance; it is not a specified assembly clearance. There is no unintended tube/equipment or tube/cable intersection. The smallest sampled equipment clearance remains approximately 4.97 mm at the unchanged pump support.
- Rod and active-contact analytic gradients and Hessians pass independent central finite-difference checks, including lateral contact forces. Maximum free-coordinate equilibrium force residual is below 1e-6 N. Axial strain remains below 0.6%.
- With paired contact disabled, the representative GPU hose returns to its fitting plane with less than 1e-5 mm lateral variation. Reflecting the complete pair about that plane reflects the solution without changing its shape. These checks specifically catch unintended lateral constraints.
- Refining a representative pair from 101 to 201 nodes, retaining the same clamped length, changes the curves by at most 0.22 mm. Refined minimum radii are 72.64/70.90 mm. Reproduce with [the verification script](../scripts/check_pvc_equilibrium.py); results are in [the verification report](../output/context-25U/pvc-3d-validation.json).
- An explicitly hypothetical 3/10/30 MPa stiffness sweep now solves the whole contacting pair and is retained in [the equilibrium report](../output/context-25U/pvc-equilibrium.json). It demonstrates sensitivity; it is not a measured material range.

All eight views (including a straight-on front tubing detail) and the native scene were regenerated and inspected. The operator has accepted the corrected front view, hose lengths and clearance (15 September 2026); commit `08beda1` records that accepted baseline. The subsequent RTX 5090 FE refinement preserves all nominal lengths and re-solves the GPU hoses for the dimensioned terminal positions, shifted by 2 mm each. P/R6 CAD, rack allocation and the open side-elbow/cage-nut concern are unchanged. The desktop Blender session was terminated without saving its in-memory state; the new scene was generated headlessly, and no desktop Blender session was left running.
