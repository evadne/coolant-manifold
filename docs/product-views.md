## R7 radiator / R7-M01 finish

[Plate perspective](../output/radiator-R7/01-plate-perspective.png) · [front](../output/radiator-R7/00-plate-front.png) · [notch](../output/radiator-R7/05-cable-notch-detail.png) · [native Blender scene](../output/radiator-R7/radiator-rack-plate-R7.blend). R7-M01 increases the four external outline corners to R5; all holes, aperture R50 corners and cable notch remain unchanged. Seven refreshed radiator views include the populated assembly, fasteners and [R5 detail](../output/radiator-R7/06-R5-corner-detail.png). The operator subsequently selected raw sheet finish for both steel parts. Existing presentation images illustrate geometry; their directional satin shader is not the production finish specification.

The [StarTech25U composite](context-25U.md) combines Q/Q-M03 and R7 with official Noctua and Koolance references, the accepted GPU/host tube routes, corrected5090 block/power geometry and provisional pump support. Nine context views and the orbitable Blender file are refreshed. Desktop Blender is not needed to generate them and should remain closed when not being used.

See [rebuild instructions](rebuild.md). Historical P studio files remain intact as source/acceptance evidence, not the current product.
